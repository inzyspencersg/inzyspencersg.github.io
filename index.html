How it's counted

SP at 10%. A single premium counts as one tenth of its value toward RP — Roy's $50,000 top-up adds $5,000 of RP-equivalent and no case, because Case: 0 means the case was already counted.

One RP figure per case. An RP line carrying several slash-separated figures is read as one case each, so the biggest-case race sees individual cases rather than a day's bundle.

Shared cases split. Two names on the FSC line means half the RP and half a case each, whether or not both names are tracked agents.

First case of the day goes by the time the post landed in the group, and only counts a post that carries a new case.

Weeks end on Sunday. Week 1 is the short launch week, Tue 8 – Sun 13 Sept; every week after that runs Monday to Sunday.

Ties split the prize. Two agents tied on most cases take $15 each, not $30 each.
